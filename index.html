Find Your Size

Accurate measurements are the foundation of finding your ideal fit

01

Measure your underband

Stand upright and wrap a soft measuring tape snugly around your ribcage, directly under your bust. The tape should feel comfortably firm without restricting your breathing.

If odd number, round up to the nearest even number

02

Measure your leaning bust

Bend forward at 90° (as if you're bowing). Let your breast tissue hang naturally and measure around the fullest part. This leaning measurement is more accurate than a standing measurement as it captures your true breast volume.

What Are Sister Sizes?

Sister sizes are bras that have the same cup volume but different band sizes. If your band feels too loose or too tight, you can size up or down the band and adjust the cup accordingly. All three sizes above have the same cup volume—pick whichever feels most comfortable.

The band does the work

Your underband provides about 80% of your support. It should feel snug and sit level. When new, fasten on the loosest hook.

Straps are secondary

Adjust your straps so they sit comfortably without digging in. They shouldn't be doing the heavy lifting.

Cup check

Your breasts should fill the cups completely without spillage, gaping, or digging in. The centre gore should lie flat against your sternum.
